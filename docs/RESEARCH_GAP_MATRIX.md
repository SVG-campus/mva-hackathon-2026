## Coverage stop rule

Discovery stops when every official requirement has a pinned source, every material decision has at least one falsifier, and a new source would not change the selected first experiment. Re-open research only when the Space commit changes, an organizer answers an open question, the real-data QC falsifies VCF-first, or a candidate mechanism triggers Track 2 review.
